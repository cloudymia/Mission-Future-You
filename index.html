Mission: Future You

Every astronaut needs a flight plan before launch day. This log is yours — fill it in as you research your future career and the schools that could get you there. It saves itself right in this browser as you go.

📋 How this journal works — read me first

This is a digital version of your Preparatory Journal. Work through the four mission phases below in any order your teacher assigns.

  • Your answers autosave in this browser on this device — use the same Chromebook each class period.
  • Hit Mark Phase Complete at the bottom of a phase to earn its badge and move your rocket forward.
  • When it's time to turn this in, use the 🖨️ Print / Save as PDF button in the final panel.

Mission Resource Library

Trusted, free sites to help you complete every phase of this mission. Each link opens in a new tab so you won't lose your place here.

🔭 Explore Careers

🧭 My Next Move ↗Take a free interest quiz and browse careers by industry. 💼 CareerOneStop ↗U.S. Dept. of Labor site with career videos, pay, and local job openings. 📊 Occupational Outlook Handbook ↗Official government data on education needed, pay, and job outlook. 🗂️ O*NET OnLine ↗Detailed profiles of the skills and tasks used in specific jobs.

🪐 Find & Compare Colleges

🎓 BigFuture ↗Search colleges by major, size, location, and cost. 🏛️ College Navigator ↗Official U.S. Dept. of Education database of every college. ⭐ Niche ↗Student reviews and rankings for campus life, dorms, and more. 📘 Peterson's ↗College profiles, virtual tours, and test-prep resources.

💵 Pay for College

🏦 Federal Student Aid ↗Where families file the FAFSA and learn about grants, loans, and work-study. 🏅 BigFuture Scholarship Search ↗Free tool to find scholarships you may qualify for.

🗓️ Testing & Deadlines

📝 College Board (SAT) ↗SAT registration, test dates, and free practice. ✏️ ACT ↗ACT registration and test dates.
💡 Don't forget your best local resources: your ICEV teacher, your school counselor, and your school or public library often have career and college info you won't find online.
